### Overview
**VSG (Vc SMS Gateway)** is a lightweight, high-performance Android utility that turns your personal Android device into an automated SMS gateway using the official Telegram Bot API. It enables seamless two-way communication: instantly relaying incoming messages to your Telegram bot and letting you dispatch outbound SMS directly through Telegram commands.

---

### Key Features
* **Real-Time SMS Sync:** Instantly forwards incoming SMS to your dedicated Telegram chat with device details, timestamps, and sender metadata.
* **Direct Outbound Dispatch:** Send SMS messages remotely using intuitive Telegram bot interactive menus.
* **Lightweight & Battery Optimized:** Built with native foreground sync services for 24/7 reliability without heavy resource consumption.
* **Privacy by Design:** Zero third-party data tracking. All requests are routed end-to-end between your device and the official Telegram API (`api.telegram.org`).
* **Auto-Start on Boot:** Keeps your gateway active seamlessly after device reboots.

### Getting Started

1. **Download APK:** Grab the latest `VSG-Release.apk` from the [Releases](https://github.com/) tab.
2. **Create a Bot:** Use Telegram's `@BotFather` to create your private bot and copy your **Bot Token**.
3. **Get Your Chat ID:** Retrieve your user or group chat ID using `@userinfobot`.
4. **Launch & Authorize:**
   * Open **VSG** on your Android device.
   * Enter your **Bot Token** and **Chat ID**.
   * Review and accept the Privacy Policy.
   * Tap **Start Service** and grant the required SMS & Notification permissions.

---

### Security & Privacy
VSG does not operate intermediary servers or databases. Your tokens, chat IDs, and SMS contents remain strictly on your local device storage.

For detailed information, please read our [Official Privacy Policy](https://nazeran.net/smsgateway-privacy).
